Apply

The cost per person for this program is Rs. 50,000 but thanks to support from the Cocoon Initiative, we are able to adopt a pay what you can model for this cohort. If you, or your organization, is able to pay the full amount, we strongly encourage you to pay.

The INR 50K includes the cost of the expedition, one night’s stay in Rishikesh, two meals on the day before and day after the expedition, and facilitated sessions on personal well-being before and during the expedition.

All participants are required to support their own travel, to and from, Rishikesh. If you would like a travel support, please list the reason while filling out the application form (read more in FAQ). Please note that support for travel is not guaranteed.

The cohort size for this program will be capped at 12 people.

If you would like to participate, please fill out this application by 21 June 2025:
https://shorturl.at/v6uSA

We will review the applications and confirm participation to selected applicants by 15th July.
