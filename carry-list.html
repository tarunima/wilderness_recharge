Guest Carry List

Here is a general checklist for Wet n Wild’s rafting trips. When on the river, Wet n Wild will have big dry bags to put your stuff in. Please do not carry anything fragile - like glass wear, While you can carry laptops to Rishikesh, we strongly urge people to not carry them on the raft. There will be a safe place to store the luggage in Rishikesh, that you don’t need on the raft.
We suggest getting rucksacks or any soft cloth bag, instead of trolley bags, as the trolley bags are difficult to fit in dry-bags that go on rafts.

  • Water bottle (We will have world class katadine water filters, OR 20L cans of packaged water, and you can refill your own bottles). We try to minimise our ecological impact and hence avoid disposable single use bottles.
  • Head Lamp
  • Personal Medication (we have a generic first aid kit)
  • Warm jacket
  • Warm head cap
  • Sunglasses with retainer strap (to prevent it falling off during rafting)
  • Sunscreen (preferably water proof)
  • Rain jacket (not needed if your jacket is waterproof)
  • Water resistant shoes / crocs
  • Personal clothing - 2 pairs of underwear, socks, inners, T-shirts, etc
  • Warm leggings
  • Sleeping bag if you have, otherwise we can provide
  • Power banks if you want to charge your phones, we will not have electricity during the rafting
  • A notebook and pencil/pen for notes and journaling.
