FAQ

This section will be continually updated.

How fit do I need to be to participate?

The program requires basic fitness. You should be able to go on easy hikes on uneven terrain as the expedition will involve walking over small stones, sand and uneven surfaces. Comfort in water is preferable but not necessary.
In case you are worried about your physical fitness, please reach out to tarunima+recharge@tattle.co.in. October is months away which is ample time to work up to the functional fitness level the expedition requires.

Can I participate if I don’t know how to swim?

Yes. You can participate even if you don’t know how to swim. While rafting we wear life jackets that keep us afloat even when we get inside the water.

What is the washroom situation during camping?

As per environment laws, there can be no permanent structures on the river beaches (yay!). The team will pitch ‘washroom tents’.

What are the food arrangements during the camp? How many meals will be provided?

On the day of arrival in Rishikesh, lunch and dinner will be arranged.
During the expedition: Breakfast, lunch and dinner will be provided by Wet and Wild expeditions. Tea/Coffee will be provided in the morning and during breakfast. Evening tea/coffee will be provided with snacks. Food will be vegetarian and non-vegetarian. There will be no alcohol on the expedition. There will be filter water during the expedition. Participants will be required to carry a bottle to refill from the water filter.

Do we have to share tents during the camp?

Usually 2 people share a tent but on a VERY special request, a single person tent can be provided.

What happens if someone falls sick or needs medical assistance during the camp?

The organisers will be carrying first aid and all the guides are certified to provide first aid in the wilderness. If an injury or illness cannot be managed on the river, the participant will be taken to a hospital through a car. There will be road support throughout the expedition.

Can the organisers also support my travel to Rishikesh?

This will only be possible in rare and exceptional cases. If you would like support for travel, please request a full waiver while filling out the application form and mention the reason for requesting travel support in the text description.

Can I drop out in the middle of the program if it gets too tough for me?

We don’t encourage participants to enter the program with the expectation to be able to leave mid-way. While there is road support, the logistics of getting people off the river safely is added work for the team and slows down the expedition. This also impacts the experience of other participants.

What will be the language of instruction for this course?

The guides will communicate in Hindi and English. Functional knowledge of one of these two languages will be necessary.

I am not an Indian national. Can I still participate?

Yes. But you will be required to submit a few additional documents to get permits. You will need to arrange for your own travel to and from Rishikesh.
