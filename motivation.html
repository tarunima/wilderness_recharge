Why A Recharge for Social Sector Leaders?

Many of us work in the social sector because of an intense desire to change the imperfect systems we find ourselves in.

Our candle burns on both ends, and it gives a lovely light.

But in giving ourselves so wholly, we risk losing sight of ourselves, and with it the hope and purity of the vision that got us started.

The five days are intended as a time capsule where you prioritize yourself over taking care of other things. And in the process experience some small but long-term change to how you approach yourself and your work.

The recharge has two components:

  • A mental reset: An opportunity to break out of our usual patterns of work and life and to observe, reflect and reconnect with the joy and lightness in ourselves.
  • Learning practical skills for personal well-being: Knowledge sharing for implementing habits for personal well-being such as financial planning, and physical and mental health management.

Why a Wilderness Based Recharge?

Time in the wilderness heightens our awareness. Spending time in nature reduces stress, anxiety, blood pressure and heart rate variability. Our stress, traumas, anxieties and fears are codified in our bodies as much as they are in our minds. Adventure activities, like river rafting, give an opportunity to understand and change our patterns by working on the mental and physical self, together.

The expedition – with the gush of rapids, beauty of the surroundings, the team work involved – will have many highs. But, the newness of the terrain, the experience of being on a river for a few hours every day, and the experience of camping outdoors will also push us into our zone of discomfort and vulnerability. In many ways, the river is a perfect metaphor for life.

The recharge will bring together pioneers, arduous workers, from the social sector. The group will contain a lot of collective wisdom from challenges met and surmounted.

While we will be paddling in the mornings, we will use the evening hours at the camp site, and the days before and after the expedition for reflection, skill sharing, story-telling and knowledge exchange. Alongside facilitated sessions, there will be time and space for self-directed reflection and unwinding.
